2. Setup — Configuring Your Profile

Before analyzing loads, complete your setup. Tap the SETUP tab in the navigation bar.

[SCREENSHOT: Setup Screen — Top]Insert screenshot of the Setup screen showing personal info fields

Personal Information

1
Enter your Full Name and Personal Email (required).
2
Optionally enter your address details.

Vehicle Settings

[SCREENSHOT: Setup Screen — Vehicle Section]Insert screenshot of the vehicle configuration fields
1
Enter your Vehicle Type / Name (e.g., Freightliner Cascadia).
2
Select your default Fuel Type (Diesel or Regular).
3
Enter your MPG (miles per gallon) — this is used in all fuel cost calculations.
4
Select your Ownership type: Owned, Financed, Leased, or Rented.
5
If Financed or Leased, enter your Monthly Payment amount.

Broker Settings

1
Enter your broker's Company Name, MC Number, and Email.
2
Enter the Broker Fee Rate (%) — this is automatically deducted in load calculations.

Profitability Goals

1
Enter your Target Weekly Revenue to set your income goals.
2
Enter your Minimum Profit Margin (%) — loads below this will flag as unprofitable.

Saving Setup

Tip: Tap SAVE SETUP at the bottom after filling everything in. Your setup data is stored locally on your device.

4. Analyze — Evaluating a Load

The ANALYZE tab is the core of the app. Use it every time a broker or shipper offers you a load.

Entering Load Details

1
Enter the PO Number (optional) for reference.
2
Enter the Offered Rate ($) — what the broker is offering to pay.
3
Enter Pickup City and Dropoff City.
4
Tap CALCULATE ROUTE MILES to automatically estimate the mileage. You can also enter miles manually.
5
Enter Deadhead Miles (empty miles to reach the pickup location).
6
Select your Fuel Type and enter the Diesel/Gas Price. Tap the GasBuddy button to look up current local prices.
7
Enter any additional costs: Tolls, Parking, Detention, 2-Man Support, Factoring Fee, etc.

Reading the Analysis

After entering your data, scroll down to see the Estimated Profitability Analysis:

FieldWhat It Means
Total MilesDeadhead + Trip + Return miles
Estimated ProfitRate minus all expenses
Revenue Per Mile (RPM)How much you earn per mile driven
Suggested CO RateMinimum rate needed to meet your profit goal
Tip: Green profit = load is worth taking. Red profit = load does not meet your minimum margin — consider a counteroffer.

Accepting a Load

1
If the load is profitable, tap ACCEPT LOAD.
2
An acceptance email draft is generated automatically — review and send it from your email app.
3
The load is added to your LOADS list as Active.

5. Sending a Counteroffer

If a load is not profitable at the offered rate, use the counteroffer feature to negotiate.

[SCREENSHOT: Counteroffer Section]Insert screenshot of the counteroffer box showing suggested rate and email draft
1
After analyzing a load, tap COUNTEROFFER if the profit is below your minimum.
2
The app calculates and displays the Minimum Rate Needed to meet your goal.
3
Enter your desired Counteroffer Rate or leave blank to use the calculated suggestion.
4
Tap DRAFT COUNTEROFFER EMAIL to generate a professional email to the broker.
5
Review the email draft, edit if needed, then tap OPEN EMAIL CLIENT to send it.
Tip: Counteroffers expire after 48 hours automatically. If not responded to, the load moves to Declined.

7. Trip Log — Tracking Your Trips

The TRIP LOG tab serves as a replacement for manual in-truck logs, recording your location and time for every trip.

Logging a Trip

1
Tap the GPS button to automatically detect your current location, or type a location manually.
2
Tap LOG START to record your departure time and location.
3
At your destination, tap LOG STOP to record your arrival.
4
The app calculates miles between your start and stop points automatically.
Tip: Allow location permissions when prompted for the GPS button to work correctly.

Downloading Trip Log

Tap DOWNLOAD TRIP LOG CSV to export your log entries as a spreadsheet for record keeping or tax purposes.

8. Reports & CSV Export

The REPORTS tab gives you detailed profit and expense breakdowns, and lets you export your load data.

Report Periods

Use the buttons at the top to select which period to view:

ButtonShows
THIS WEEKCurrent week's loads and profit
PREV WEEKLast week — useful at week changeover
THIS MONTHCurrent month's performance
PREV MONTHLast month — useful at month-end
YEARFull year to date
ALLAll-time summary

Report Cards

Each period shows an expandable card with a full breakdown including Revenue, Gas Cost, Mileage Cost, Vehicle Payment, Insurance, Broker Fees, Tolls, Parking, and Estimated Profit. It also shows whether you are over or under your break-even point.

Exporting to CSV

1
Select the period you want to export using the filter buttons.
2
Tap EXPORT [PERIOD] TO CSV.
3
The file downloads to your device and can be opened in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app.
Tip: Use the CSV export regularly for tax preparation, bookkeeping, and risk analysis of your load history.

9. Tips & Best Practices

Daily Workflow

1
When a broker calls with a load offer, open the ANALYZE tab immediately and enter the details while on the phone.
2
Review the profit analysis. If it meets your goal, accept. If not, use the counteroffer feature.
3
Use the TRIP LOG at every pickup and delivery to keep accurate records.
4
Check the DASH daily to track how you're performing against your weekly goals.

At Week / Month End

Use PREV WEEK or PREV MONTH on both the Dashboard and Reports tab to review your completed period before it rolls off the current view.

Data Safety

Important: All your data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the app or switch phones without a backup, your load history will be lost. Regularly export your data to CSV as a backup.

Subscription Management

To manage or cancel your subscription, go to your device's Google Play Store → Subscriptions. You can cancel at any time before the next billing date.

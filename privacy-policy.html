Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

KB Cooper Enterprises ("we," "us," or "our") operates the KB Cooper Load Analyzer mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data you enter into the App — including load details, calculations, notes, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using local device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

2. Data Storage

All app data is saved locally on your device. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted. We recommend keeping backups of any important records outside the App.

3. Ads

The App does not display advertisements and does not use any advertising networks or tracking SDKs.

4. In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions in the future. Any such transactions will be processed through the Google Play Store and are subject to Google's privacy policy and terms of service. We do not collect or store payment information.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not currently integrate any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or data collection services. Future updates may include purchase management services (such as RevenueCat), which may collect anonymized transaction data as described in their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for use by adults 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to external servers, your information remains under your control at all times. We recommend using your device's built-in security features (PIN, biometrics) to protect your data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
